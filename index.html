About me

I'm a PhD candidate in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Oklahoma, working with Dr. Kimball Martin.
Previously, I received an M. Math. from the Indian Statistical Institute.
I do research in Algebraic and Computational Number Theory. Some of my areas of interest are:
  • Mod \(\ell\) Congruences of Modular Forms
  • \(\ell\)-adic Galois Representations
  • \(\ell\)-adic Modular Forms
I'm also pretty decent in SageMath, Haskell, Lean 4, Rust, Go, Python, and a few other programming languages.
